What it keeps in order

Each row names the control as it appears in the extension, so what you read here is what you will click.

Sync Full Library Check for deleted items
Sync Full Library imports every image and video you generated, liked or not. The first run reads the whole library and takes a few minutes; later runs add only what is new since the last sync, usually well under a minute. While you browse grok.com, new items are captured passively. Check for deleted items reads the whole library again and hides media deleted on Grok, keeping any copies you saved to Local; a sync does this check by itself when the last one is more than 7 days old. The Only model-generated option leaves your uploads out. Video quality is tracked as SD, HD or 1080p, and a video is never downgraded.
Tags Manage Tags
Tags are your Grok collections, synced both ways: a tag you add in the Manager appears on Grok, and vice versa. Older Grok folders are read too. There is no tag limit, Free or Pro.
Categories Set Category
A nested category tree that lives only in the Manager. Drag cards onto a category to file them, or set a category for a whole selection. Free has 3 categories; Pro has no limit.
Rating Notes
One to five stars and free-text notes, kept locally. Rate or categorize many items at once from the selection bar.
Search Minimum rating
Search prompts, notes and IDs. Filter by type, video quality, minimum rating, tags, and Cloud (still on Grok) or Local (saved on your disk). Sort newest, oldest, by prompt, by rating, by most children.
Grid List Nest Prompt
Four gallery views: Grid; List with the columns you choose; Nest, which shows a source image with the videos made from it; and Prompt, grouped by prompt. Group by day, week, month, prompt, rating or Grok conversation, with conversation titles. A slider sets thumbnail size.
Recover prompts
New items that arrive without a prompt get it filled in automatically after a sync. For older items, Recover prompts reads your Grok conversations to fill in missing prompts and links videos to their source images. It can be stopped and resumed.
Select All Download
Select many items with Shift or Ctrl-click, a drag box, or Select All. Then manage tags, rate, set a category, or download through Chrome, flat or into folders by category.
Save to Local Save All to Local Pro
Copies media into a folder on your computer, so it stays viewable after Grok deletes it. Save a selection or everything. The run is paced to stay within Grok's limits: one file at a time, an automatic pause with a countdown when Grok asks for one, and Cancel at any point.
Backup & Restore Backup Now Pro
A daily automatic backup of tags, categories, ratings and notes into your media folder, kept for 30 days. Manual backups are kept forever. Restore from any of them.

Where your data lives

Everything stays in the browser. The library, tags, categories, ratings and notes are stored in IndexedDB. Saved media and backups go to a folder you pick on your own disk.

The only server call is license validation. There is no account and no analytics inside the extension. Prompts and media never leave your device.

It reads Grok's responses in your grok.com tab. The only thing it writes back to Grok is tag edits, to your Grok collections. Media, prompts, conversation titles, ratings, categories and notes are never written to Grok.

Questions

How is Grok Media Manager different from Grok Media Downloader?
Grok Media Downloader gets files out of Grok: it is a downloader. Grok Media Manager organizes the library: it syncs what you made, lets you tag, rate, categorize, search and group it, and keeps copies on your disk. They are two separate extensions and work side by side.
Where is my data stored?
In your browser (IndexedDB) and, for saved media and backups, in a folder you pick on your own disk. The only server call is license validation. There is no account and no analytics inside the extension; prompts and media never leave your device.
What does the Manager change on Grok?
Only your tags. Tags are two-way synced with your Grok collections, so a tag you add or remove in the Manager is added or removed on Grok too. Media, prompts, conversation titles, ratings, categories and notes are never written back to Grok.
What is free and what needs Pro?
Free: full library sync, passive capture, unlimited tags, ratings, notes, search, filters, the four views, grouping, conversation titles, prompt recovery and Chrome downloads, with up to 1,000 items shown in a gallery view and 3 categories. Pro removes the item and category limits and adds Save to Local and Backup & Restore.
