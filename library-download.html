How to Download All Your Grok Imagine Favorites at Once Whole Library Download, step by step

Every image and every video you made in Grok Imagine, saved to your computer in one run and grouped into one folder per conversation, named with the prompt that created them.

Short answer

Install Grok Media Downloader, open grok.com/library and click the extension icon. Leave the Download Source on 📦 All Generated Media (by Conversation) to get everything, or pick 🌟 Favorite for only the items you saved. Click 1. Generate List and click OK in the Download Summary that follows (or click 2. Start Download later). The files land in your Downloads folder under grok_downloads/, one folder per conversation.

The free version downloads images from the last 7 days. PRO adds videos and your full library.

Before you start: Grok changed its media backend in July 2026

Grok migrated to a new conversation-and-asset model. Since then the Favorites list, which is Grok's Saved page, no longer receives newly generated media on many accounts, so a favorites-only download can miss your recent work. Most people who want to download "all their favorites" really want everything they made. That is what the 📦 All Generated Media (by Conversation) source reads: your whole library, saved or not. It has been the default source since v2.5.1.

The endpoints that older extension versions depended on no longer back the Saved and media library views, which means versions before v2.5.0 cannot detect newly generated images and videos. Version 2.5.0 is a complete adaptation to the new backend, and Whole Library Download is what that new API made possible. On 12 and 13 November 2026 Grok also folded Imagine into the main Grok app, and versions before 2.6.3 greyed out their buttons as a result. Use version 2.6.3 or newer; here is how to fix the greyed-out buttons.

1. Update

Install or update from the Chrome Web Store: version 2.6.3 or newer

2. Reload

Reload the extension in chrome://extensions, then refresh your Grok tab

3. Regenerate

Generate your download list once more so the new naming rules, merged tags, and prompts recovered from the generating conversation apply

Step by step: download your whole Grok Imagine library

Once the extension is installed, the download itself takes two clicks. The screenshots show the English popup; the extension uses the same layout in every language.

  1. 1

    Install Grok Media Downloader and pin it

    Open the Chrome Web Store page and click Add to Chrome. Then click the puzzle-piece icon in the Chrome toolbar and pin Grok Media Downloader so its icon stays visible. If you already have it, open chrome://extensions and check that the version is 2.6.3 or newer. Chrome updates extensions on its own within a few hours; to update right away, turn on Developer mode on that page and click Update.

  2. 2

    Open grok.com/library

    Sign in to Grok and open grok.com/library. Since version 2.6.3 the extension works on any grok.com page, and the Library is a good place to start. If your account still has the Imagine Saved page (grok.com/imagine/saved), that works too. On a tab that is not grok.com, the popup disables its buttons and shows "Please open grok.com (Library or Imagine Saved page) and try again." with a Go to Grok Library → link.

  3. 3

    Click the extension icon to open the popup

    From top to bottom the popup shows the status panel, the media types to download, the 🎯 Download Source selector and the two buttons that do the work: 1. Generate List and 2. Start Download. Under 🎬 Media Types, tick 🖼️ Images. The 🎬 Videos (Pro) box needs a PRO license; the free version downloads images only.

  4. 4

    Choose what to download

    Pick one of the three sources in the Download Source selector:

    • 📦 All Generated Media (by Conversation) is your whole library, one folder per conversation. It is the default and the right choice for a full backup. In the free version it covers the last 7 days, and the popup says so under the selector. PRO removes the limit.
    • 🌟 Favorite is only what you saved in Grok. Since July 2026 this list can miss newly generated media, and the popup shows a warning when you pick it.
    • 🏷️ Tag is one Grok tag, also called a collection. Pick the tag from the list that appears.

    With PRO you can also narrow the Favorite or All Generated Media scan with the Date Filter, either a date range or the last N hours. The Tag source has no date filter.

  5. 5

    Click 1. Generate List

    The extension scans the source newest-first. For the whole library it also checks which quality tiers each video has and prepares the conversation folders, and the button shows which stage is running. When the scan finishes, a 📊 Download Summary dialog lists the images and videos to download, with videos broken out by 1080p, HD, 720p and SD, and how many are already done. It ends with "Start download?": OK starts the download right away; Cancel closes the dialog, shows the counts in the status panel and leaves 2. Start Download enabled. Anything already in your download history is not counted again. If you later change a filename or metadata setting that needs prompts, the popup asks you to regenerate the list.

  6. 6

    Start the download

    Click OK in the Download Summary. If you cancelled it, click 2. Start Download and the same summary appears again. When more than 250 videos are queued, the summary also says that the extension will pause after every 250 videos. The progress bar shows how many files are finished and roughly how long is left. Keep the browser and the grok.com tab open while it runs. To pause, click ⏹ Stop Download; the button then reads ▶ Continue Download and picks up where it stopped. The pause after every 250 videos lasts 3 minutes by default; you can set it from 1 to 60 minutes under ⚙️ Settings → ⏱️ Rate Limit Cooldown. After the pause the download resumes by itself.

  7. 7

    Find your files

    Files go to Chrome's Downloads folder, inside a grok_downloads folder with these sub-folders:

    • conversations/ for All Generated Media, one folder per conversation
    • images/ and videos/ for Favorite downloads in Standard mode
    • projects/ for Favorite downloads in Group by Project mode
    • tags/ for Tag downloads, one folder per tag
    • stream/ for Stream Capture (PRO)

    You can change each path under ⚙️ Settings. Paths are relative to Chrome's download folder.

  8. 8

    Run it again whenever you have new work

    Each source keeps its own download history, so the next Generate List shows only what is new and nothing is downloaded twice. Archiving the whole library never marks your favorites as already downloaded, and the other way round. If you deleted files and want them again, see "I lost my files" below.

How folders and filenames are decided

A whole-library archive is only useful if you can find things in it a year from now.

Complete library scan

Reads your full asset list instead of the favorites subset. Nothing you generated is left behind because you forgot to save it.

Conversation folders

One folder per conversation, so a source image and every video generated from it land together instead of scattered across a flat directory.

Real prompt filenames

Images use their own generation prompt; a video uses its own animation prompt, or borrows the source image's when it has none.

Independent history

Whole-library downloads keep their own deduplication record. Archiving everything never marks your favorites as already downloaded.

Each conversation folder is named yyyymmdd_prompt_shortID. When a conversation has no title, the extension uses its prompt instead of labelling the folder "untitled".

For the files inside, open ⚙️ Settings and choose a 📝 Filename Format: Grok Native gives grok_image_YYYYMMDD_id.jpg, Prompt Based gives img_YYYYMMDDHHmmss_prompt.jpg, and Custom Template lets you combine the tokens {date} {time} {datetime} {id} {shortid} {prompt} {quality}. Turn on 📋 Save Metadata to write a .json file with the prompt, resolution and other details next to each image and video. All of this is free.

Grok stopped sending prompts with the media list in September 2026. Since v2.5.6 the extension recovers each prompt from the conversation that generated the item and keeps it in a persistent cache, so a later whole-library regenerate takes seconds instead of minutes. Prompts are fetched only when your filename or metadata settings use them. Assets you uploaded yourself, or that carry no generation info, have no prompt or conversation on the server; those are filed under their asset ID. More in Save Grok Imagine videos with their prompts.

Large libraries

A whole-library scan has to read every asset you have. These are the parts that keep it going on big accounts.

Adaptive throttling and 429 backoff

During Generate List, requests that Grok answers with HTTP 429 or a 5xx server error are retried, and the scan slows down automatically. Libraries of 8,000+ assets can be scanned end to end without breaking off midway.

Concurrent quality checks

Video quality is resolved with bounded concurrency and a live counter, cutting a step that used to take several minutes down to roughly one.

Batched conversation titles

Titles are fetched in batches for only the conversations actually needed, instead of paging through your whole conversation list.

Early-stop date filter (PRO)

The scan runs newest-first and stops as soon as it passes your date window, so a narrow filter does not walk through your entire library.

1080p support

Video quality: SD, HD/720p and 1080p

Downloads always take the highest quality Grok has for each video. HD Upgrade, a PRO feature, asks Grok to make a higher-quality version first.

📦

SD

Baseline output. HD Upgrade can raise it to HD/720p; this step does not need SuperGrok Heavy.

🎥

HD / 720p

The standard upgrade target, one step up from SD.

💎

1080p

Highest tier. Requires SuperGrok Heavy on your Grok account and an explicit opt-in.

Opt-in, never surprise-billed

1080p upgrades need SuperGrok Heavy, so the "Upgrade HD/720p to 1080p" checkbox is off by default. With it unchecked the extension sends no 1080p requests at all and only performs SD to HD upgrades, but the confirmation dialog still tells you how many videos could go to 1080p, so they never silently disappear. If your account turns out to lack the entitlement, the extension detects it once and stops issuing further 1080p requests instead of spinning.

Upgrade scans that converge

Grok returns the HD media URL for freshly generated videos inconsistently, which made the same video read as SD on one scan and HD on the next, so repeating "upgrade the last N hours" gave a different answer every time. Since v2.5 the extension remembers the highest quality ever resolved per video and never downgrades on an incomplete read. Records expire after 30 days, are capped in count, and self-clear if a remembered URL turns out to be dead. Videos that couldn't be checked at all because of rate limiting are excluded and reported rather than mistaken for SD.

Upgrade the whole library, not just favorites

HD Upgrade has its own Upgrade Source selector, Favorite or 🌐 Whole Library, so you're not limited to upgrading videos you remembered to save. The confirmation dialog breaks your library down by SD, HD/720p and 1080p and states how many will be upgraded in this run, at roughly 8 seconds per video. A whole-library scan can be stopped partway. After upgrading, run Generate List and Start Download again to fetch the better versions. The full walkthrough is in Download Grok videos in HD.

Troubleshooting

The problems we hear about most, with the fix that resolved them in practice. Most trace back to Grok's 2026 changes rather than to a broken setting.

Generate List and Start Download are greyed out
First check that the active tab is a grok.com page; on any other site the popup disables both buttons and offers a Go to Grok Library → link. If you are on grok.com and the buttons are still grey, your extension is older than 2.6.3. On 12 and 13 November 2026 Grok folded Imagine into the main Grok app, and older versions no longer recognised the page. Chrome updates extensions automatically within a few hours; to update now, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and click Update. Full walkthrough: Grok download button missing after update.
Generate List finds nothing, or misses my newest images
Check the Download Source selector. If it still says Favorite, switch to All Generated Media (by Conversation). Since Grok's July 2026 change, new generations are no longer added to Favorites, so a Favorite scan can legitimately come back empty. The whole-library source is free for everyone (images from the last 7 days; PRO covers videos and the full library) and has been the default since v2.5.1. Then click Generate List again. More background in Grok favorites not loading? Back up your library.
Only images were downloaded, no videos
That is how the free version works: it downloads images only. Video downloads, shown as 🎬 Videos (Pro) under 🎬 Media Types, need a PRO license. After you activate PRO in the popup's License section, tick Videos, run Generate List again and click Start Download; your download history skips the images you already have. See pricing.
The download paused by itself
That is a planned pause, not an error. The extension pauses after every 250 videos, waits 3 minutes by default and then resumes by itself; the Download Summary warns about this before a run with more than 250 videos. You can set the wait from 1 to 60 minutes under ⚙️ Settings → ⏱️ Rate Limit Cooldown. You don't need to click anything; leave the browser and the grok.com tab open.
Downloads start failing after thousands of files
On very long runs Grok can start refusing downloads partway through, for example with HTTP 403 errors after thousands of videos. Reload the grok.com tab, open the popup and click Start again. Your download history skips everything that was already saved, so only the missing files are fetched.
HD Upgrade says every video is already upgraded
Same cause as an empty list: the upgrade scan reads the list of the selected source, and the Favorite list is incomplete. Set the Upgrade Source to 🌐 Whole Library, regenerate the list, and run the upgrade scan again. Since v2.5 the scan converges: repeated runs return the same result, and 1080p is tracked as its own tier so a 720p file is never mistaken for the top quality. HD Upgrade is a PRO feature.
Filenames, folder names or metadata.json have no prompt in them
Grok stopped sending the prompt with the media list in September 2026. Update to v2.5.6 or newer, which recovers each prompt from the conversation that generated the item, then regenerate the list once: names are decided during the Generate List step, so a list made by an older version keeps the old names. In Settings, choose the "Prompt Based" filename mode if you want prompts in filenames. A persistent cache keeps recovered prompts, so later regenerates take seconds.
A tag I created in Grok is missing, or a tag downloads only one file
Grok runs two tag systems side by side since its August 2026 migration, and each is missing items the other has. Versions 2.5.2 to 2.5.4 read only the new one. Update to v2.5.6 or newer: it reads both and merges them, so tag lists and tag contents are the union of the two. After updating, regenerate the list for the tag.
I lost my files and the extension says everything is already downloaded
Each source keeps its own download history. For the whole-library source, open the popup, expand Clear Actions and click Clear Conversation History; confirm the dialog. The button "Reset Download History" applies to the Favorite source only, and the Tag source has its own "Clear Tag History" button. Clearing a history does not delete anything on Grok or on disk; it only lets the next run download those items again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Downloading your whole Grok Imagine library

Can I download all my Grok Imagine favorites at once?

Yes. Open grok.com/library, click the Grok Media Downloader icon, choose 🌟 Favorite as the Download Source, then click 1. Generate List and 2. Start Download. Because Grok's Saved list stopped receiving new media on many accounts in July 2026, most people get a more complete result with 📦 All Generated Media (by Conversation), which downloads your whole library whether or not you saved an item. The free version downloads images, and for the whole-library source it covers the last 7 days; PRO adds videos and removes the time limit.

What is Whole Library Download?

Whole Library Download is a download source introduced in GrokMediaDownloader v2.5 that fetches every generated image and video in your Grok Imagine library, not only the items you marked as favorites or filed under a tag. In the extension popup it appears in the Download Source selector as "All Generated Media (by Conversation)", alongside the existing Favorite and Tag sources. Because it reads your complete asset list rather than a curated subset, it is the right choice when you want a full local archive of everything you have generated. Files are grouped into one folder per conversation, so the images that started a thread and the videos generated from them end up together instead of scattered across a flat download directory.

How is Whole Library Download different from Favorite and Tag downloads?

The three sources answer different needs and are fully independent of one another. Favorite downloads only the items you have saved in Grok, which keeps the set small and curated. Tag downloads everything filed under a specific Grok tag or folder, and organizes output under tags/{tagName}/. Whole Library ignores both filters and covers your entire generated library, organizing output by conversation instead. Each source keeps its own separate download history, so downloading your whole library never marks your favorites as already downloaded, and vice versa. You can run all three over time without one polluting the deduplication records of another, which was a common source of confusion before v2.5.

Does the free version download videos?

No. The free version downloads images from every source, and from the whole-library source it covers the last 7 days. Video downloads, the full library without a time limit and the date filter are part of PRO, a one-time $4.99 purchase. Generate List, the three sources, filename templates, the .json metadata file and download history are free.

Where does the extension save the files?

In Chrome's Downloads folder, inside grok_downloads. The whole-library source writes to grok_downloads/conversations with one folder per conversation; Favorite downloads go to images and videos (or projects in Group by Project mode), and Tag downloads to tags. You can change every path under Settings. Everything runs locally in your browser, and files go straight from Grok to your disk.

How are folders and filenames decided?

Each conversation becomes one folder, named using the pattern yyyymmdd_prompt_shortID. When a conversation has no title, the extension falls back to that conversation's prompt rather than labelling the folder "untitled", which keeps folders meaningful when you browse them months later. Since September Grok no longer sends prompts with the media list, so from v2.5.6 the prompt is recovered from the conversation that generated the item: a video uses its own animation prompt, or borrows the source image's when it has none. A persistent prompt cache stores what it recovers, so a whole-library regenerate drops from minutes to seconds. Prompts are fetched only when your filename or metadata settings actually use them, and after you change one of those settings the popup asks you to regenerate the list. If you prefer machine-style names, the Grok Native filename mode produces grok_image_YYYYMMDD_id. metadata.json also records userId, and video filenames match the generated list, carrying the same short-id suffix.

Why did my extension stop finding newly generated Grok media?

In July 2026 Grok migrated to a new conversation-and-asset media backend. The older endpoints that previous extension versions relied on no longer back the Saved and media library views, so versions before v2.5.0 cannot see images and videos created after that migration. GrokMediaDownloader v2.5.0 is a full adaptation to the new backend and restores detection of newly generated media. After updating, open chrome://extensions, reload the extension, and refresh your Grok tab. You should also regenerate your download list once, so the merged tag results and the prompts v2.5.6 recovers from the generating conversation are picked up; prompt-based filenames and the conversation folder rules are written during the Generate List step and are not applied retroactively to a list built by an older version.

Can I filter a whole-library download by date?

Yes, with PRO. The Whole Library source supports the same date filtering as the Favorite source, covering both a recent-hours window and an explicit date range; the Tag source has no date filter. The scan walks your library newest-first and stops early once it passes the end of your selected window, so filtering by date does not force a scan of your entire library before results appear. This matters for large accounts: combined with adaptive throttling and 429 rate-limit backoff, libraries of 8,000 or more assets can be scanned end to end, but a narrow date filter finishes far sooner. The Generate List button reports progress through scanning, video quality resolution, and folder preparation so you can see which stage is running. Without PRO, the whole-library source covers the last 7 days.

What does 1080p support add in v2.5?

Version 2.5 recognizes 1080p as a distinct quality tier throughout the extension. Downloads automatically select the highest available quality, preferring 1080p over HD over the original file. Scan statistics break out how many videos sit at SD, HD/720p, and 1080p rather than collapsing everything into SD and HD. The HD Upgrade feature (PRO) can raise videos one tier at a time, from SD to HD and from 720p or HD to 1080p. Upgrading to 1080p requires a paid Grok plan, specifically SuperGrok Heavy, so it is behind an explicit opt-in checkbox that is off by default. With the box unchecked the extension sends no 1080p requests at all.

Why did repeated upgrade scans previously return different results?

Grok's server returns the HD media URL for freshly generated videos inconsistently: querying the same video twice within seconds could return the field once and omit it the next time, even though the resolution field stayed stable. That made the same video look like SD on one scan and HD on another, so running "upgrade the last N hours" repeatedly produced a different result every time. Version 2.5 adds an upgrade-only quality memory that records the highest quality ever resolved for each video and never downgrades it when a later query comes back incomplete. Records expire after 30 days, are capped in count, and are cleared automatically if a remembered URL turns out to be dead.

Is Whole Library Download available in the free version?

Yes, for images from the last 7 days. Since v2.5.1 the download source selector is free for everyone, because nobody should be stuck on a source that cannot see their own media. Free users can select "📦 All Generated Media (by Conversation)" and download the images from the last 7 days; the extension states this window up front rather than silently truncating your results. GrokMediaDownloader PRO, a one-time $4.99 purchase with a lifetime licence, adds video downloads, removes the time limit so you can archive your complete library, and unlocks date filtering (a specific range or a recent-hours window). PRO also covers Stream Capture, Story Mode export, unlimited Video Gen Queue, Project-Based Download, HD and 1080p upgrade, and history export and import. Everything runs locally in your browser and files go straight from Grok to your disk; the only outside connection is the license check with Kario Studio's license server.

If I run it again, will it download the same files twice?

No. Every source keeps a download history, and Generate List leaves out what is already recorded there, so a second run only fetches new items. This also means you can stop a long download and continue later without starting over. If you want to download everything again, clear that source's history first.

Known limitation: assets you uploaded yourself, or that carry no generation info, have no prompt or conversation on the server. Those are filed under their asset ID.
