Privacy Policy

Grok Media Manager · Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kario LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Grok Media Manager Chrome extension ("the Extension") and its page at grokmedia.kario-studio.com/manager/. The short version: your library stays on your device; when you use Pro, the Extension sends us your license key and a one-way code that ties the license to one Grok account (never the account ID, your e-mail address or your name); and we do not track, sell or share your data.

1. Data stored on your device only

The Extension keeps everything it manages in your own browser and on your own disk. We cannot see any of it.

  • IndexedDB (in Chrome): media metadata read from Grok (Grok item IDs, media URLs, prompts, model name, dimensions, file size and dates) together with the tags, categories, ratings and notes you create.
  • chrome.storage: your settings, sync state and cached conversation titles; the Grok account your library belongs to (its Grok user ID and a masked hint such as jo•••th@example.com); and the state of your Pro license's seat.
  • A folder you pick on your disk (File System Access API): media files you save with Save to Local, and metadata backups made by Backup & Restore. A backup also records the Grok user ID and the masked hint of the library's account, so a restore can tell which Grok account the backup belongs to.

2. What the Extension reads from Grok, and what it writes

Inside your own signed-in grok.com tab, the Extension reads your media lists, your tags and collections, your conversation titles and prompts, and downloads media from assets.grok.com. All of this happens between your browser and Grok; none of it is sent to us.

Each library belongs to one Grok account: the one signed in on grok.com when you first sync. Before it syncs or writes tags, the Extension checks inside your grok.com tab which Grok account is signed in. It asks Grok's own account endpoint (/rest/auth/get-user) and compares Grok's x-userid cookie. Only the account ID and a masked hint (for example jo•••th@example.com, or a masked X handle or first name) leave the grok.com page; your full e-mail address and name do not. If grok.com is signed in to a different Grok account, syncs and tag changes wait until you switch back. None of this is sent to us.

The only thing the Extension writes to Grok is your tag edits: a tag you add or remove in the Extension is added to or removed from your Grok collections. Media, prompts, conversation titles, ratings, categories and notes are never written to Grok.

3. The license service

Pro is activated and validated through our license API at api.kario-studio.com. One license works with one Grok account, on any number of computers and Chrome profiles. For that, the Extension sends:

  • your license key;
  • a one-way code derived from the Grok account ID of your library (HMAC-SHA256, keyed with your license key). The account ID cannot be read back from it, and it is the same on every computer where you use the same Grok account;
  • a label made of "Grok account" and that code's last 4 characters, which the Extension's License panel also shows, so that you and our support can name the same seat;
  • the Extension's version number.

The Extension never sends your Grok account ID, your e-mail address or your name. It checks the license once a day; that check also updates the seat's "last seen" date. The reply (plan, expiry date and the purchase e-mail) is cached in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome may synchronize between your own signed-in browsers; the seat state stays in the browser profile and is not synchronized. No media, prompts, tags or notes are ever sent to the license service. Like any web server, the license service sees the IP address of each request; it is used only to limit abuse (rate limiting) and is kept briefly in counters and server logs, not with your license.

To move your license to another Grok account, reply to your license e-mail or write to support@kario-studio.com. A seat that has not been used for 60 days is also released when the license is next activated for another Grok account.

4. Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects your e-mail address and payment details; we never see your card details. Stripe's handling of your data is described in Stripe's privacy policy. We keep the purchase e-mail and the license record so that we can validate your key and help you if you lose it. With the license record we keep its seat: the one-way code, its label, the Extension version, the activation date and the last-seen date, so that one license serves one Grok account.

5. What the Extension does not do

  • No analytics and no tracking inside the Extension.
  • No ads.
  • No remote code: everything that runs is shipped with the Extension.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.

The page at grokmedia.kario-studio.com uses Google Analytics to measure visits to the website itself. That measurement is limited to the website and does not reach the Extension. The walkthrough video on that page is embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and is loaded only after you press play; from then on Google's privacy policy applies to the player.

6. Chrome permissions and why they are needed

  • storage, unlimitedStorage: the local library and your settings.
  • tabs, scripting and host access to grok.com, *.grok.com and *.x.ai: reading your Grok data in your own tab, checking which Grok account is signed in there, and writing your tag edits there.
  • downloads: the Download feature.
  • sidePanel: the side panel next to grok.com.
  • alarms: retrying tag write-backs to Grok when they fail, and checking the Pro license once a day (the same check confirms its seat).
  • Host access to api.kario-studio.com: talking to the license service. A page script that runs only on the /api/success page of api.kario-studio.com (and, for older purchase links, of grok-media-manager-api.vercel.app): the one-click Activate in Grok Media Manager button after a purchase, nothing else.

7. Removing your data

Uninstalling the Extension deletes its IndexedDB database and its chrome.storage data. Media files and backups you saved to your own folder are ordinary files on your disk; they stay there until you delete them.

Uninstalling does not delete the seat record kept with your license on our license service. It is deleted when our support releases it at your request, or when, after 60 days without use, the license is activated for another Grok account. Our license log keeps a short record of seat changes (date, a short prefix of the one-way code, and the Extension version) so that support can answer questions about the license.

8. Children's privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
